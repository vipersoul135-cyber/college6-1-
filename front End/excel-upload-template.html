🚀 How to Upload

Step-by-Step Process:

  1. Download the Template (optional but recommended)
  2. Fill in the Excel Data
    • Enter all required columns (S.No, Reg Number, Name)
    • Add optional columns (Email, Phone, Photo, etc.) as needed
    • Save your Excel file
  3. Go to Admin Panel → Students Data
  4. Click "Upload Excel" button
  5. Select Your Department and Year from dropdowns
  6. Choose Your Excel File and click "Upload"
  7. Review the Preview and confirm
⚠️ Important Notes:
  • Department and Year are selected in the upload form (not in Excel)
  • Reg Number must be unique within the same department and year
  • Photo URLs must be publicly accessible (not local files)
  • Existing students in the same department/year will be updated
✅ After Upload:
  • Students appear in Students Data page
  • Photos display as circular thumbnails
  • Click "View Details" to see full profile
  • All data searchable and filterable

❓ Common Issues

Q: Why isn't my photo showing?
A: The photo URL must be publicly accessible. Make sure the link doesn't require login or has CORS restrictions.
Q: How do I add photos for existing students?
A: You can update them individually by going to Students Data, clicking "View Details", and using the edit functionality (if available), or re-upload with photo URLs.
Q: Can I use base64 images for photos?
A: Yes! Format: data:image/jpeg;base64,/9j/4AAQSkZJRg...
Q: What happens if I upload duplicate Reg Numbers?
A: The system updates existing records instead of creating duplicates. The serial number is preserved if already set.
